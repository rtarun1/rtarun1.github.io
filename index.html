Projects

Traversability-Aware Indoor Navigation

2026

Used diffusion foundation model priors to segment the traversable floor and estimate depth indoors, and wrote a Numba-based GPU MPPI controller that is soft-constrained to plan trajectories only within the traversable region.

Slides

Transparent Object Grasping Using Modified SILICA

2026

Trained SILICA with CLIP conditioning to predict glass objects and glass scenes separately, greatly reducing false positives. The resulting segmentation and depth feed NVIDIA GraspGen for grasp generation, with an RRT* planner to reach and pick up the object.

Slides

Omnidirectional Vision-LiDAR Calibration and Fusion Framework

2025

Wrote a ROS 2 driver and image stitcher for the Insta360 camera, and designed a synchronization method from scratch to align its untimestamped images with a Livox MID-360 LiDAR. Also built a targetless extrinsic calibration pipeline for a LiDAR and five RealSense cameras using LightGlue. This setup was used to collect over 2 TB of multi-sensor data in a shopping mall.

Slides

Surfing Scout the Quadruped

2025

Designed a 12-servo controller PCB with micro-ROS firmware in C, and wrote a ROS 2 driver with body-to-leg inverse kinematics so the quadruped can reach target body orientations.
